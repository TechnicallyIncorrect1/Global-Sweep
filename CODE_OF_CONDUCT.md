# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes include a welcoming attitude, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility and apologizing if necessary.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may remove anyone violating this code.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/).
